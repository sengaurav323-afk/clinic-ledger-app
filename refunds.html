Cancellation and Refunds

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. Free Trial

New accounts begin with a free trial period. You may cancel at any time during the trial with no charge.

2. Cancelling Your Subscription

Your Monthly or Yearly plan is paid for a fixed period and does not renew automatically, so no further charge is made unless you choose to pay for another period. You can stop using Curevona at any time, and your current paid period remains active until its end date. Cancelling or stopping use does not by itself trigger a refund; refund requests are considered only under the eligibility conditions in section 3 below.

3. Refund Eligibility

We offer refunds in the following cases:

  • You were charged more than once for the same billing period (duplicate charge).
  • A technical failure on our end prevented you from accessing the Service you paid for.
  • You submit a refund request for your first paid subscription charge within 7 working days of the charge date.

Refunds are generally not provided for partial use of a billing period after this window, or for change-of-mind cancellations once the paid period has substantially elapsed.

Amounts already paid for a subscription are non-refundable after cancellation, except where a refund is required by applicable law or is approved under the refund eligibility conditions stated in this policy.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us through our Contact Us page. Include your registered email address, payment date, amount, and transaction reference or payment ID if available. Please do not submit sensitive payment credentials such as card numbers. We will review your request and aim to respond within 7 working days.

5. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method, where supported. Refunds typically reflect within 7 working days after processing, although the actual time may vary depending on the payment provider and the customer’s bank or payment method.
