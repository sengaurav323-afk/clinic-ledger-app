About

Curevona is a digital clinic management platform designed to help doctors and clinics manage their everyday administrative work more efficiently. Our goal is to simplify routine clinic operations by bringing essential information and workflows into one convenient digital platform.

What Curevona Does

Today, Curevona helps clinics keep patient records organised, record payments by fee type, track expenses, and review the day, week or month in simple reports. It works on a phone or a desktop, and each clinic's records stay within its own account, subject to the platform's access controls and privacy practices.

Our Mission

We aim to help healthcare professionals spend less time on repetitive administrative tasks and more time focusing on their patients. Curevona is being developed with an emphasis on simplicity, usability, reliable record management, and accessible technology for clinics of different sizes.

Our Vision

Our vision is to make digital clinic management easier to adopt for healthcare providers by offering practical tools that support organised workflows, clearer records, and more efficient day-to-day operations. We continue to improve Curevona with a focus on user needs, responsible handling of information, and a better experience for clinic teams and, as patient-facing tools are added, for their patients too.

Founder: Gaurav Sen

Curevona was founded by Gaurav Sen with the vision of making clinic management simpler and more accessible through practical digital tools. The initiative focuses on reducing repetitive administrative work, improving the organization of clinic information, and helping healthcare providers adopt technology that supports their daily operations.
